One line: a single original dad joke, at most 140 characters. `main.py`
writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line).

The joke, as a dad would tell it at the dinner table:

> I asked my calendar for a joke. It said, 'I'd love to — but my days are numbered.'
